## Tools (kept out of the pack by owner decision)

- Circle-of-Willis arteries: the TopCoW 2024 winning model of team CLAIM (Charite Berlin; nnU-Net v2 ResEnc-M with the Skeleton Recall loss, five folds, best and final checkpoints averaged), weights extracted from the organisers' docker release (Zenodo record 15665435, `Team_CLAIM_topcow_seg.tar.gz`) and run with nnU-Net 2.8.1 / torch 2.11.0+cu128 on 2x RTX 5080 (`scripts/vessels/cow_segment.py`). Checkpoints read with `torch.load(weights_only=True)`. The team's YOLO locator is not used (its weights need unrestricted unpickling); the circle is located by a first pass of one fold. Licence: the model is trained on TopCoW data, whose terms are "Open use. Must provide the source. Use for commercial purposes requires permission of the data owner." (OpenData Swiss); the nnU-Net code is Apache-2.0. Citation: Yang K et al., Benchmarking the CoW with the TopCoW challenge, arXiv:2312.17670.
- Post-processing and QC: `scripts/vessels/cow_post.py`, `scripts/atlas/mr_contours.py`.

## QC rules

- Rule 0 mapping: segmentation grid identical to the NIfTI made from the pack's DICOM (`mr_convert.py`, affine read-back < 1e-3 mm); sampled voxels NIfTI affine vs DICOM IPP/IOP/PixelSpacing < 0.5 mm (oblique series included); every published outline re-rasterised and compared with its mask (Dice >= 0.9).
- Rule 1 fragments: labels are kept only inside a teaching box around the circle's landmarks (30 mm lateral to the carotid termini, 20 mm in front of the Acom, 35 mm behind the basilar top, 25 mm below it, 30 mm above); the largest connected part of each artery; parts under 3 mm3 or on fewer than 2 slices dropped.
- Rule 2 TOF signal (instead of HU): median signal inside an artery, scored against the head (q = (median - head median) / (head 99.5th percentile - head median)), at least 0.80 for the main arteries and 0.40 for the communicating arteries and the third A2 (the lowest expert values were 1.03 and 0.57; calibration below).
- Rule 4 side and contacts: lateral arteries on their side of the midline (centroid, and >= 90 % of voxels; 75 % for a PCA or Pcom, which start at the midline basilar tip); the right ACA right of the left ACA; each artery joins the ones it must (MCA and ACA their ICA or the Acom, PCA the basilar or its Pcom, a Pcom both its ICA and PCA, the Acom both ACAs, a third A2 the Acom); otherwise it is not shown.
- Sub-segments: A1/A2 split at the Acom junction, P1/P2 at the Pcom junction (P1 must also reach the basilar), M1/M2 at the first division of the MCA into two branches of >= 6 mm; geodesic distances inside the mask; no junction, no split.
- Visual check: axial and coronal MIPs of every case with every published outline (`mr_render_cow.py`), recorded in `WORK_MR/cow_visual_check.json`.

## Brain anatomy on brain MRI

2497 outlines on 90 series (ISLES diffusion studies: on the DWI series; the ADC map of the same acquisition was the input and shares the grid).

- Tool: GOUHFI 2.0 (Fortin et al., arXiv:2601.09006; code and weights Apache-2.0, Zenodo 17920473), a contrast- and resolution-agnostic nnU-Net (ResEnc-L, domain randomisation as SynthSeg), fold 0 of the segmentation model (35 FreeSurfer-style labels) and of the cortex-parcellation model (62 DKT gyri), run on the two RTX 5080s (`scripts/atlas/brain_segment.py`, `mr_run_brain.py`). Images that are not skull-stripped were first masked with the brain class of TotalSegmentator's licence-free `total_mr` task (dilated 2 mm). SynthSeg 2.0 was the first choice; its weights are only distributed inside FreeSurfer, whose licence is accepted by downloading, so it was not used.
- Structures: frontal, parietal, temporal and occipital lobes (cortex parcels plus the nearest white matter), insula, cingulate gyrus, precentral and postcentral gyri (the central sulcus between them) and superior temporal gyrus, lateral ventricles (with temporal horns), third and fourth ventricles, caudate, putamen, globus pallidus, thalamus, hippocampus, amygdala, brainstem, cerebellar hemispheres. Thin gyri on the 2 mm diffusion grids usually fail the outline check and are not shown there.
- QC (`mr_brain_contours.py`): mapping and outline-vs-mask (rule 0), largest part and minimum size (rule 1), CSF signal by sequence instead of HU (rule 2: ventricles darker than white matter on T1, FLAIR and DWI, brighter on T2 and ADC), coverage (rule 3), side of the midline and anatomical order (rule 4), and next to a tumour outlined by the dataset's experts nothing within 10 mm is shown, on every series of that patient (rule 5: the anatomy there is displaced and cannot be checked); on grids coarser than 1.5 mm (the diffusion series) a structure is shown only if its validation at 2 mm (below) reaches Dice 0.65 and 85 % of what the grid allows (rule 6: amygdala, postcentral_gyrus, third_ventricle not shown there); visual check of every case (`mr_render_brain.py`, `WORK_MR/brain_visual_check.json`).

### Validation against manual labels (20 OASIS-TRT-20 brains, Mindboggle-101)

## Known limitations

- Unreviewed machine outlines. Small communicating arteries (Acom, Pcom) are the least reliable: the model may show a thin Pcom the experts called not seen, or miss one they saw (validation above). Variant questions are graded only on expert labels.
- Only the arteries near the circle are outlined; the vertebral arteries, distal branches and the veins (dural sinuses are suppressed on TOF) are not.
- Brain outlines follow the scan as it is: tumours, infarcts and enlarged ventricles shift or deform them, and lobar borders come from the gyral pattern learned by the model, not from sulci traced by a radiologist.
