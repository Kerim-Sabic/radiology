## 7. Contract notes for the app

- `image_px` = [column, row], zero-based, (0,0) = centre of the first pixel. RTSTRUCT targets are `patient_lps_mm`; SEG, LIDC and CBIS
  targets are `image_px` on the referenced SOP (frame 1). Rings are stored **implicitly closed** (first vertex not repeated). Nested rings
  on one plane are split into `polygons` and `holes` by the even-odd rule (RTSTRUCT and mask-derived contours alike).
- Mask tracing: `skimage.measure.find_contours` at level 0.5 on the zero-padded mask (returns (row, col); converted to [col, row]) and
  `approximate_polygon` (0.25 px for SEG, 0.5 px for the CBIS mask). The contour therefore runs along pixel edges of the mask.
- LIDC outlines are the raw XML edge-map points (integer pixel indices = pixel centres); per LIDC convention they lie just outside the
  nodule. Each reader's outline is a separate plane entry with `reader` set ("LIDC reader N" = reading-session order in that XML; the
  same N is not the same radiologist across patients).
- `acquisition` values are strings exactly as in the header; multi-valued elements are joined with `\`. A value that differs between
  instances of a series is reported as `varies across N instances (...)` (only LIDC-IDRI-0217's PA+lateral DX series has this, for
  PatientOrientation; its displayNote says to read orientation per instance). Keys are omitted when absent from the DICOM.
- `spacingCalibrated` is true only for CT/MR PixelSpacing; radiographs here have ImagerPixelSpacing and/or PixelSpacing without
  PixelSpacingCalibrationType, so all are false (displayNote explains). The CBIS mammogram has no spacing at all.
- Window presets: CT by region (chest: soft tissue 40/400, lung -600/1500, mediastinum 50/350, bone 400/1800; abdomen, extremity, spine,
  head variants); MR/DX/CR from the file's WindowCenter/Width; otherwise "Auto (p1-p99)" computed over a subsample of stored values
  (after rescale) excluding pixels at the series' minimum and maximum value (background/saturation) — only the CBIS mammogram needed this.

## 8. Failures, skips and caveats

- **PROSTATEx-Seg-Zones**: there is no NBIA collection of that name; the zone SEGs are third-party-analysis series inside collection
  `PROSTATEx` whose CollectionURI is `https//doi.org/10.7937/tcia.nbb4-4655` (sic, malformed in the API) = "PROSTATEx Zone Segmentations"
  (Meyer et al. 2020). The other PROSTATEx SEG set (10.7937/TCIA.2019.DEG7ZG1U, high-resolution whole-gland) was not used.
- **LCTSC**: LCTSC-Train-S1-010 was downloaded first and rejected (heart contour only ~57 mm cranio-caudal); 12 RTSTRUCTs were compared
  before choosing S1-012 (starter), S2-003 and S3-006 (body). LCTSC RTSTRUCT files carry SOPInstanceUID == SeriesInstanceUID as distributed.
  The S2 CT is a 4DCT average (ImageType DERIVED\SECONDARY\REFORMATTED); its role is set to `diagnostic` by selection.json because it is
  the planning CT the contours reference (roleNote explains). All contours lie exactly on their slice planes (residual 0.0000 mm).
- **COVID-19-AR**: dates are shifted (DICOM de-identification code 113107, intervals preserved). CT locators (1 image) are role `scout`;
  MIP/MPR series were skipped except one labelled coronal MPR (`vendor_reformat`, linked `same_volume` to its axial source by identical
  FrameOfReferenceUID + AcquisitionDate/Time). The contrast chest CT in `body` has no reference targets (no source vessel annotations exist);
  its use for great-vessel navigation rests on its range (to the lower neck) and ContrastBolusAgent 'CONTRAST'.
- **LIDC-IDRI**: annotations come from the official `LIDC-XML-only.zip` (TCIA collection page); the DICOM-LIDC-IDRI-Nodules SEG/SR
  derivative was not needed. All LIDC dates are the placeholder 2000-01-01, so the CT-radiograph interval is unknown and `relativeDays`
  is null. Cross-reader nodule grouping is computed by this pipeline (method in provenanceDetail); malignancy ratings are reader suspicion
  scores, not diagnoses. No LIDC CR/DX reads were converted.
- **Soft-tissue-Sarcoma**: PET series, the MR series resampled to PET space (`Aligned_*toPET_BOX`) and their RTSTRUCTs, and `RTstruct_PET`
  were excluded. CT is the CT component of the PET/CT (roleNote). Contours exist separately per series (CT, T1, T2FS/STIR); no registered
  links were created. EchoTime/RepetitionTime are absent from these MR headers.
- **C4KC-KiTS**: 14 small arterial series were probed with getSingleImage for slice thickness; KiTS-00015 (3 mm) chosen. Kidney and Mass
  segments do not overlap (0 px), so 'Kidneys' excludes the tumour. SegmentAlgorithmType SEMIAUTOMATIC -> provenance source_manual (the
  collection describes human segmentation); the raw type is in provenanceDetail.
- **ReMIND**: SEGs have no ReferencedSeriesSequence/DerivationImageSequence; the reference series (3D T1 post-contrast, named in the SEG
  SeriesDescription) is declared in selection.json and verified by identical FrameOfReferenceUID. Ventricle/cerebrum SEGs use a finer
  0.49 mm x 0.5 mm grid offset 0.25 mm from the T1 slices: one SEG frame per T1 slice (nearest, ties to the lower projection) is mapped
  through patient coordinates, max frame-to-slice distance 0.25 mm. Ventricles and cerebrum are AUTOMATIC (source_automated); the glioma
  is MANUAL. All ReMIND dates are the placeholder 1982-12-25. TE/TR are absent from the headers. The three ReMIND MR series carry three different FrameOfReferenceUIDs as distributed, so they are linked `independent` (no shared frame asserted) although acquired in one pre-operative session; the SEGs share the T1 series' FrameOfReferenceUID. The cerebrum target JSON is ~3 MB.
- **Spine-Mets-CT-SEG**: 17 vertebra segments T1-L5 (SEMIAUTOMATIC); lesion classifications are not converted.
- **CBIS-DDSM**: images are Secondary Capture objects (Modality MG), no PixelSpacing, PatientOrientation 'CC' (invalid as orientation);
  the film label appears mirror-imaged in the pixel data as distributed (no flip applied). ROI from the paired '_1' series identified as the
  full-size binary image; pathology (MALIGNANT, BI-RADS 4) is source-stated from the official CSV. The ROI is a level-set segmentation
  (Lee et al. 2017) -> source_automated.
- **expansion** (12 new patients, none used in another pack): all 57 unused LCTSC RTSTRUCTs were screened (every one has the five OARs);
  S1-008, Test-S2-202, S3-007 and S3-011 were picked for ~2.5-3 mm slices, plausible extents and three institutions. KiTS tumour side from
  the SEG 'Mass' outline centroid (LPS +x = patient left): KiTS-00045 x = +59.3 mm -> LEFT; KiTS-00204 x = -93.3 mm -> RIGHT. LIDC
  cross-reader grouping never merges two outlines of the same reader (LIDC-IDRI-0111 has two adjacent nodules that a plain union-find
  joined; it now yields 4 separate 4-reader nodules); rebuilding chest-pairs with this rule left its 8 target geometry files
  byte-identical. Spine-Mets 13800 has SliceThickness 3 mm in the header although its SeriesDescription says '.5mm' (header used).
  ReMIND-014's tumour SEG is on a 0.7 mm grid; one frame per T1 slice was used (max 0.49 mm from the slice plane).
- **more-ct-xr** (21 new patients). *Pediatric-CT-SEG*: 36 subjects screened (age from the CT header, coverage, ROI extents, thickness);
  4 chosen (1 y M and 5 y M chest-abdomen-pelvis; 12 y F and 15 y M abdomen-pelvis). Every contoured RTSTRUCT ROI became a
  `contour_set` target with `layer: "atlas"` and a `category`; ROIs listed in the structure set but without contours (out of scan range or
  sex-specific) produce no target and are named in `unassessedNote`. classIds reuse earlier ones (anatomy:heart, anatomy:lung_left/right,
  anatomy:esophagus) and add e.g. anatomy:liver, anatomy:spleen, anatomy:kidney_left/right, anatomy:bladder; 'Spinal Canal' is
  anatomy:spinal_canal (not spinal_cord). The 2 mm series are, per the collection, mostly cubic-spline reformats of thin-slice CT.
  *MIDRC-RICORD-1C*: the official MD.ai export (`midrc-ricord-1c-da-other-json.zip`, TCIA collection page) contains only GLOBAL,
  STUDY-scoped labels (5,440 annotations, 0 with geometry) - there are no boxes or polygons to convert. Labels are therefore stored as
  `image_label` targets (one per label group: Bear, Dragon, Tiger) with the label text verbatim (including the source's double space in
  'Mild Opacities  (1-2 lung zones)'). Six single-image studies with unanimous labels were chosen to span Typical (2), Indeterminate (2),
  Atypical (1) and Negative (1). RICORD-1C is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial) per the API. *COVID-19-AR*: four more same-day
  radiograph + axial CT pairs (two CT PE 3 mm, two venous chest-abdomen-pelvis 3 mm). *LIDC-IDRI*: 4 more CT + DX subjects (interval
  unknown). *LCTSC*: S1-007, S2-004 (4DCT average; role set to diagnostic as in `body`), Test-S3-202. The six RICORD radiographs are
  distributed as JPEG Lossless SV1 (`1.2.840.10008.1.2.4.70`) and are kept byte-for-byte (another decoder fixture). The pediatric 'Skin'
  (body outline) targets are large (6.8-9.7 MB JSON each, ~380k vertices as distributed in the RTSTRUCT); targets/ totals ~77 MB, which is
  why the atlas layer should be loaded lazily. LIDC-IDRI-0149's PA and lateral DX have different matrix sizes (declared in volumeIssues and
  displayNote; the validator reports it as a warning).
- Radiographs without PatientOrientation in the header (starter CR 16406503, LIDC-IDRI-0078 CR, LIDC-IDRI-0134 CR, the CT localizers) have no
  `PatientOrientation` key and a displayNote saying so; nothing is inferred. The CBIS mammogram's value 'CC' is reported as found and flagged.
- `public/packs/index.json` is updated by merging entries by id: entries of packs not built here (e.g. `vessel-drafts`) are kept as found.
- Disk: at start C: had only ~4.3 GB free, so raw downloads are kept as the getImage zips (read in memory, never extracted); exploration-only
  downloads were removed afterwards. Raw store at `C:\Users\kerim\radiology-data\raw` (override with RADIOLOGY_RAW_DIR).

## 10. .gitignore note (do not commit large packs)

Only `starter` is meant to be committed. Suggested entries:

```gitignore
# learning packs: only the starter pack is committed; others are rebuilt with scripts/data/packs.py
public/packs/*/dicom/
public/packs/*/thumbs/
!public/packs/starter/dicom/
!public/packs/starter/thumbs/
```

(Git cannot re-include a file whose parent directory is excluded by a pattern like `public/packs/*/`, so ignore the `dicom/` and `thumbs/`
subdirectories rather than the pack directories.) `pack.json`, `targets/` and `lessons.json` of non-starter packs are small enough
(< 5 MB total per pack except chest-pairs/neuro-msk targets ~1-6 MB) to commit if wanted; without `dicom/` they only describe data that
`python scripts/data/packs.py all <packId>` re-downloads and rebuilds bit-identically (JPEG-LS/J2K/RLE output is deterministic for the
pinned codec versions; the validator checks sha256s).
