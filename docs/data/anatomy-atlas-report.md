## QC rules

- Rule 0 mapping: CT voxels mapped to LPS through the NIfTI affine vs the DICOM IPP/IOP/PixelSpacing of the matched SOP (< 0.5 mm); radiographs: rasterised outline vs bilinear-upsampled model output on the original grid (Dice ≥ 0.9).
- Rule 1 fragments: largest 3D component only (lung lobes ≤ 2, colon ≤ 4, small bowel ≤ 8, duodenum/portal-splenic ≤ 2, pulmonary veins ≤ 4; extra parts ≥ 5 % of the volume); costal cartilages keep every part ≥ 0.5 ml (≈20 separate cartilages by anatomy); CT rings/holes < 10 mm² dropped; < 0.3 ml or < 2 slices → dropped. CXR: largest region (diaphragm ≤ 2), rings < 0.2 % and structures < 0.1 % of the image dropped.
- Rule 2 density (median HU): ribs/vertebrae/flat bones ≥ 150, humerus/femur ≥ 100 (fatty marrow cavity — deliberate class-specific deviation from the 150 HU example), lung lobes/trachea/main bronchi ≤ −300, soft-tissue organs and muscles −50…250, vessels/kidneys/heart −50…250 (−50…450 in contrast studies). Hollow organs with gas or faeces (stomach, bowel, colon parts, bladder, oesophagus) and costal cartilage are not density-checked. Note: veins carrying undiluted contrast (often > 600 HU) are dropped by this rule.
- Rule 3 coverage: a structure reaching the first/last slice (≥ 10 % of its largest slice area there) or the image edge is labelled "(partially imaged)"; it is dropped if < 20 % of the typical volume (median of complete adult instances in this dataset). Complete structures with ≥ 3 reference instances are dropped if < 20 % or > 4× typical. Paediatric CTs are not compared with adult volumes.
- Rule 4 order: every left/right pair per shared slice and by 3D centroid; ribs per slice on the correct side of the midline (spinal cord, else vertebrae); lateral organs/bones/muscles by 3D centroid; vertebrae C1→S1 strictly craniocaudal (outliers dropped); aortic parts (ascending anterior + right of descending, arch above both, abdominal below thoracic) and colon parts (ascending right of descending).
- Rule 5 (CXR): structure centroid inside the lung bounding box expanded by 25 %; left/right pairs and heart-left-of-spine checked; when PatientOrientation is absent the note says the side is not verifiable from the header.

## Known limitations

- Unreviewed machine outlines: rib/vertebra numbering can be off by one level, bowel loops can be confused, structures at the edge of the field of view are partial. QC catches flips, slice mix-ups, gross mislabels and fragments, not subtle boundary errors.
- Radiograph outlines are projection regions at the model's 512-pixel resolution (about 4–6 original pixels per edge).
