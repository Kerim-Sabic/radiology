## Conversion and LPS/RAS handling

- Every instance listed in the source pack's `pack.json` is read with pydicom; SOP/Series UIDs and IPP are checked against pack.json; HU = stored x slope + intercept (int16). Slices are sorted by IPP . n (n = IOP row x column cosine); spacing must be uniform (<= 0.01 mm) and IPPs collinear along n.
- A SimpleITK image is built with index (i, j, k) = (DICOM column, DICOM row, sorted slice), origin = IPP of the first sorted slice (centre of its first pixel, LPS mm), spacing = (PixelSpacing[1], PixelSpacing[0], slice step), direction columns = (IOP[0:3], IOP[3:6], n), and written with `sitk.WriteImage(..., .nii.gz)`. ITK keeps LPS internally and its NIfTI writer stores qform/sform in RAS (x and y negated); voxel order is unchanged. Read back with nibabel, `diag(-1,-1,1,1) @ affine_RAS` equals the DICOM-derived LPS affine (differences below).
- TotalSegmentator writes each class mask in the geometry of the input NIfTI. For every mask the shape and affine were compared with `ct.nii.gz`; a mismatch would trigger nearest-neighbour resampling with SimpleITK (it never did).
- Contours: per original slice (slice k -> SOP Instance UID via the IPP sort, re-verified from the affine), 4-connected components >= 4 px, `skimage.measure.find_contours(mask, 0.5)` on a zero-padded array, (row, col) -> `[col, row]` image_px (pixel-centre convention, (0,0) = centre of the first pixel), closed rings, `approximate_polygon` tolerance 0.35 px, enclosed gaps stored as `holes`.

## Pack

- `public/packs/vessel-drafts/pack.json` + `targets/`: 55 contour_set targets (image_px) and 10 slice_interval targets; 1116.4 kB (excluding any `lessons.json`, which this pipeline does not write).
- The manifest has empty `subjects/studies/series/spatialLinks`; every target references a series UID and SOP Instance UIDs of the body / chest-pairs pack(s), which must be loaded too (the app indexes targets across packs by series UID).
- Validated with `scripts/data/zod_check.mjs` (PackManifest + every TargetGeometry), with the geometry checks in `write_pack.py` (every SOP UID belongs to the referenced series, rings closed, all points inside the image) and end-to-end by `verify_targets.py` on all 65 written targets: each plane's SOP UID is resolved to its DICOM file, the polygons are rasterised on that file's pixel grid, compared with the model mask (Dice) and their centroid mapped to LPS with the file's own IPP/IOP/PixelSpacing vs the mask centroid through the NIfTI affine; slice intervals list exactly the instances whose IPP . normal falls in range. A one-slice SOP mix-up would show as a residual equal to the slice step (2.5–3 mm); a row/column swap or flip as tens of mm. Read straight from the DICOM pixels, tracheal outlines average -878 to -775 HU (air) and the five great-vessel outlines 92 to 1165 HU (blood pool; the lower values are systemic arteries on pulmonary-angiogram timing and the vein on the non-injected side, the highest the vein carrying undiluted contrast), i.e. the outlines sit on the intended anatomy.

## Known limitations

- These are segmentation-model outputs on 2.5–3 mm slices; partial-volume and contrast-mixing artefacts (dense injection on one side, unopacified vein on the other) can shift or truncate vessel outlines, especially the left brachiocephalic vein and the origins of the arch branches.
- The five-vessel rule treats wall-to-wall contact between two labels as acceptable (distinct labels), because the arch branches commonly abut each other at 3 mm slice thickness; such contacts are listed above.
- The five-vessel interval runs from the first slice above the arch to the last slice before the brachiocephalic trunk divides or a brachiocephalic vein ends, so it ranges from 2 slice(s) (`ar-16434411`) to 10 slices (`ar-16434363`); the single textbook 'five-vessel' image corresponds to somewhere inside it. A short interval usually means one brachiocephalic vein is labelled only just above the arch (e.g. an unopacified vein on the non-injected side).
- Series reconstructed with a sharp lung kernel have noisy mediastinal soft tissue (`lidc-0217`, kernel LUNG), and CT pulmonary angiograms (`CT PE CHEST` studies) enhance systemic arteries only moderately; outlines there are more uncertain.
- Context structures (aorta, SVC, trachea, oesophagus, right carotid/subclavian) are included as separate targets over their whole segmented extent; they were not part of the five-vessel rule except the aorta (arch top).
- Nothing was reviewed by a human. Before using any of this for assessment-grade scoring, a reviewer should check each outline and level and set `review` / `provenance` accordingly.
