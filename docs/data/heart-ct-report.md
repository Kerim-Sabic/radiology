# Heart and great vessels on chest CT (`heart-ct`)

Generated by `scripts/data/heart_ct_report.py` from `docs/data/heart-ct-cases.json` (written by `scripts/data/heart_ct_build.py`).

## Summary

- 36 chest CTs of adults, 2466 DICOM slices, 3051 files, 101.5 MB (budget 250 MB / about 3,000 files; hard stop 400 MB / 5,000 files). 437 dataset outlines and 110 AI outlines; 36 lesson cases, 129 tasks.
- Source: TotalSegmentator CT dataset v3.0.0, Zenodo record 22688904 (re-read from the Zenodo API on 2026-11-09: version 3.0.0, 1,939 CTs, `Totalsegmentator_dataset_v300.zip`, 37,415,622,057 bytes, licence cc-by-4.0, md5 0728beea76475e92cdb3160e228265d1). Read with HTTP range requests: the 35 MB central directory once, then `meta.csv` and, per case, only the members the pack uses (about 1 GB in total; the Zenodo rate limit is handled by waiting for `Retry-After`). Every member is checked against its zip CRC-32.
- Pixel data: the dataset's CTs are 1.5 mm isotropic NIfTI. Each case is cropped in plane to the body and in z from about 20 mm above the top of the aortic arch (up to the arch branches, never into the neck) to 12 mm below the heart, every second slice kept (3 mm), HU clipped to -1024..3071, stored as unsigned 12-bit values with Rescale Intercept -1024, JPEG-LS lossless. Cropping and thinning are why 36 cases fit the budget (full 1.5 mm stacks would be several GB).
- Identity: PatientName blank, PatientID `HCT-<hash>`, StudyDescription `CT chest`, neutral lesson titles `Patient <code> · Chest CT`. Age and sex come from the dataset table. The reading-room texts are checked against a leak pattern (dataset names, diagnosis words) at build time.

## Selection

Candidates came from `meta.csv`: adults, `study_type` among heart, aortic valve, atrium, coronary arteries, thoracic aorta, angiography of the chest and routine chest types; pathology `bleeding` and `unclear` excluded. 333 candidates were screened from their own segmentations: heart, aorta, pulmonary vein and both venae cavae all non-empty, heart at least 3 voxels from the field-of-view edges in plane and 4 slices from the ends, heart volume 150 to 1200 ml (275 had all five structures, 233 passed the field of view). From those 51 candidates were chosen to balance institute (the dataset is about 80 % Basel, so other hospitals were taken whenever eligible), scanner (all the eligible GE cases chosen; no eligible Philips or Toshiba case), study type and pathology tag; 36 were kept after the contrast check and the size budget.
Contrast is measured, not assumed: median HU of the dataset's aorta outline over the heart's levels, >= 150 HU = contrast-enhanced (as in docs/data/vessel-drafts-report.md). The dataset has no contrast field.

- Contrast-enhanced: 27. Not enhanced: 9, of which clearly unenhanced (aorta <= 60 HU): 5; faint or late contrast (60 to 150 HU): 4.
- Institutes: A 2, C 3, J 28, K 3. Manufacturers: ge 2, siemens 34.
- Pathology: the dataset's table records only a coarse tag (`vascular`, `tumor`, ...) with an organ system, never aneurysm, dissection or pulmonary embolus. No case is called a dissection or an embolus. 9 cases carry the tag `vascular`; 4 of them (s1133, s0825, s0822, s0936) also have an aorta whose largest inscribed circle is 43 to 52 mm, and are taught as 'a widened aorta' with the measurement, not as an aneurysm or dissection (`s1133`, 52 mm with heterogeneous content in the descending aorta on the images, is the clearest). A pulmonary-embolus case cannot be identified from the dataset's metadata, so none is included (the `pe-cta` pack covers it).

## Labels

**Dataset outlines** (`provenance: source_automated`): heart, aorta, left atrial appendage, pulmonary veins, venae cavae, brachiocephalic trunk, both brachiocephalic veins, carotids and subclavians, those that lie inside the cropped chest. The dataset's paper (Wasserthal et al., Radiology: AI 2023, Data Annotation) says: first segmentations from public models or an iteratively retrained nnU-Net, then manually reviewed and corrected where necessary, supervised by two physicians with 3 and 6 years of body-imaging experience; version 3.0.0 refined many labels. `provenanceDetail` says exactly that: model-assisted and radiologist-verified, not drawn from scratch, not re-reviewed here. Only the largest connected part is kept (up to four for pulmonary veins, two for brachiocephalic veins) and the left/right order of paired vessels is checked on the centroids.

**AI drafts** (`provenance: ai_draft`, layer `atlas`, source Horalix, never graded): left ventricular myocardium, left and right atrium, left and right ventricle, pulmonary arteries.

- Tool: MONAI model-zoo bundle `wholeBody_ct_segmentation` v0.1.9 (SegResNet, 1.5 mm weights `model.pt`), licence Apache-2.0 (bundle LICENSE), trained on the TotalSegmentator dataset v1 (Zenodo 6802614, CC BY 4.0): the model the anatomy atlas already uses. Normalisation over the whole volume, inference inside the heart's box + 30 mm. torch 2.11.0+cu128, MONAI 1.6.1, nibabel 5.4.2.
- GPUs: two RTX 5080 (driver 610.88), one worker process per GPU (`heart_ct_ai.py <gpu> <cases>`): 36 cases, mean 1.0 s and at most 3.1 s per case including loading and normalising the volume (the network itself takes under a second). The first GPU was shared with another job (8.8 GB in use).
- **Not run: TotalSegmentator's licensed tasks `heartchambers_highres` and `coronary_arteries`.** The tool's own offline licence check (`has_valid_license_offline`, which never returns the number) reported `missing_license` for `totalseg-home` and for every other TotalSegmentator home folder on this machine, and both tasks exit with the tool's licence message without it. The configuration file was never opened and no workaround was attempted. Consequence: there are **no coronary-artery outlines**, and the chambers come from the licence-free bundle above. To add them: enter the licence number with the tool's own `totalseg_set_license`, then run the two tasks on `work/heart-ct/<case>/ct_crop.nii.gz` (see `scripts/data/heart_ct_ai.py`).
- Rules (as the atlas's chamber QC): chambers only on contrast studies; one connected part; plausible volume (atria 20-250 ml, ventricles 40-350 ml, muscle 60-400 ml, pulmonary arteries 15-160 ml inside the crop); at least 90 % inside the heart outline dilated by 2 voxels; blood-pool median >= 120 HU and >= 40 HU above the muscle; left atrium left of the right atrium, left ventricle left of the right ventricle and of the right atrium, left atrium and left ventricle behind the right ventricle; pulmonary artery in front of the aorta's centre and near the top of the heart, >= 60 HU. The model's aorta was not used (the dataset's aorta outline is better).

## Quality control by eye

Every case was rendered with `scripts/data/heart_ct_qc.py` (four axial levels, coronal and sagittal reformats rasterised from the stored polygons) and all 36 sheets were looked at. Outlines sit on the right structures and the right side in all 36. Weak points seen and left in (unreviewed AI drafts): the AI left ventricle is small on the four-chamber slice of `s1372` and `s0825`; the dataset's heart outline in `s0911` has a small connected anterior part. The chamber thresholds were relaxed once (blood-pool 150 -> 120 HU and 'above the muscle' rule; pulmonary artery 100 -> 60 HU) and the newly included chambers were checked again by eye.

Dropped because they failed a rule:
- AI structures: s0604 atrium_right (blood pool 83 HU (< 120)); s0604 ventricle_right (blood pool 88 HU (< 120)); s0753 atrium_right (blood pool 72 HU (< 120)); s0753 ventricle_right (blood pool 79 HU (< 120)); s1372 atrium_right (blood pool 81 HU (< 120)); s1372 ventricle_right (blood pool 80 HU (< 120)); s1395 atrium_right (only 55% inside the heart); s0911 myocardium (only 35% inside the heart); s0911 atrium_left (only 10% inside the heart); s0911 ventricle_left (only 32% inside the heart); s0911 atrium_right (only 0% inside the heart); s0911 ventricle_right (only 0% inside the heart); s0911 pulmonary_artery (-735 HU (< 60)); s1022 atrium_right (blood pool 111 HU (< 120)); s0825 atrium_right (blood pool 102 HU (< 120)); s0825 ventricle_right (blood pool 94 HU (< 120)); s0822 atrium_right (blood pool 81 HU (< 120)); s0822 ventricle_right (blood pool 92 HU (< 120)); s0773 atrium_right (blood pool 74 HU (< 120)); s0773 ventricle_right (blood pool 79 HU (< 120)); s0659 atrium_right (blood pool 88 HU (< 120)); s0659 ventricle_right (blood pool 115 HU (< 120)); s0871 atrium_right (volume 0 ml outside 20-250); s0881 atrium_left (volume 0 ml outside 20-250); s0115 atrium_right (blood pool 105 HU (< 120)); s0115 ventricle_right (blood pool 110 HU (< 120)); s0457 atrium_right (blood pool 97 HU (< 120)); s0457 ventricle_right (blood pool 98 HU (< 120)); s0936 atrium_right (blood pool 100 HU (< 120)); s0936 ventricle_right (blood pool 105 HU (< 120))
- Chamber model without usable output on a contrast study (nothing returned, or outlines outside the heart): s0911, s0879, s0871, s0931, s0881. Their dataset outlines are kept; they get no chamber lesson.
- Chamber model not run on studies without a clear contrast injection: s0197, s1199, s1162, s1337, s0085, s1084, s1254, s0811, s1157 (blood and heart muscle cannot be told apart).
- Dataset outlines not in the cropped chest (gated studies end below the arch; the right carotid and subclavian are often cut off): see the table.
- No case was dropped for failing QC; 15 of the 51 candidates were not used to stay inside the size budget or because a better-matched case of the same kind was available.

## Lessons

Tasks per kind: aorta-size 8, arch 33, chamber-anterior 3, chamber-posterior 4, chamber-thickwall 3, chambers 10, contrast 32, vessels 36. Naming tasks are `exploratory_self_review` against the dataset outlines (model-assisted, not authoritative); chamber tasks are self-review with the AI outlines only as a late hint; contrast and aorta-size tasks are choice questions answered from the measured HU and the largest inscribed circle. Nothing is made free (`server/free.ts` untouched).
