Who makes Radiology Monster?

Radiology Monster (radiology.monster) is made by Horalix, a medical AI company in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. Horalix builds AI for medical imaging, including an AI-assisted echocardiography workflow for clinician review.

What is Radiology Monster?

A radiology reading workstation in the browser. You scroll whole CT, MRI, X-ray, mammography and ultrasound studies from 600+ real, de-identified patients, mark what you see, and are graded against the expert reference on the exact slice. There are 1,700+ graded image tasks, anatomy drills, on-call shifts and certificate exams in three levels.

Each task names the page of Learning Radiology (Herring, 5th ed.) that explains it. Radiology Monster is not affiliated with or endorsed by its publisher or authors.

Who is it for?

Medical students and radiology residents who want to practise reading real scans, including for exams. Professors can open a class with one join link and follow their students' study days, exam results and certificates, never their answers or payments.

Where do the cases come from?

From public research collections whose licences allow reuse, including commercial use. The studies are de-identified, and every case in the app names its source and licence. The largest collections are:

  • Breast-Lesions-USG (BrEaST), The Cancer Imaging Archive · CC BY 4.0
  • ChestX-ray14, NIH Clinical Center (Wang et al., CVPR 2017) · unrestricted use
  • ISLES 2022 stroke MRI (Zenodo) · CC BY 4.0
  • GRAZPEDWRI-DX paediatric wrist radiographs (figshare) · CC BY 4.0
  • CBIS-DDSM mammography, The Cancer Imaging Archive · CC BY 3.0
  • FracAtlas fracture radiographs (figshare) · CC BY 4.0
  • Lausanne TOF-MRA Aneurysm Cohort (OpenNeuro) · CC0 1.0
  • LIDC-IDRI and LCTSC chest CT, The Cancer Imaging Archive · CC BY 3.0
  • COVID-19-AR chest imaging, The Cancer Imaging Archive · CC BY 4.0
  • And smaller sets from The Cancer Imaging Archive (C4KC-KiTS, Pediatric-CT-SEG, Lung-PET-CT-Dx, Spine-Mets-CT-SEG and others), HC18 fetal ultrasound, FUMPE CT pulmonary angiography and Digital Knee X-ray (Mendeley Data).

How is my answer graded?

Your mark is compared with the reference outline or box published with the collection. It counts only if it lands on the finding, on the right slice and the right side. Multiple-choice questions are checked automatically. Grading is deterministic: the same mark always gets the same grade.

What is AI and what is expert?

Findings come from the reference annotations published with each collection, made by its radiologists and clinicians or under their supervision. Only those references grade case answers and certificate exams.

The anatomy layer (organs, vessels, bones and airways named on every scan) is drawn by AI from Horalix and labelled AI wherever it appears; it has not been reviewed by a radiologist. Anatomy drills use it too, marked AI, and the Expert labels only switch leaves it out.

Can I use it to diagnose patients?

No. Radiology Monster is for education and practice only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for clinical decisions.
